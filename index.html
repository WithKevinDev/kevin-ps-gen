Frequently Asked Questions
What is Kevin Hub?
Kevin Hub is a tool that lets you generate private servers for popular Roblox games instantly.
How do I use it?
Simply click the "Gen Now" button on any game card above, and it will take you to the private server generator.
Are all the generators free?
It depends on the game. Some games (like Steal An Egg) have free private servers, while others (like Blox Fruits, Fisch, or Bee Swarm) require the game developer's paid private server fee to be paid on Roblox.
Do I need to join Discord?
While not required, joining our Discord (discord.gg/luascript) gives you access to support, updates, and exclusive generators.
